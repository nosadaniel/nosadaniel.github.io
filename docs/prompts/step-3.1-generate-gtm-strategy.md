You are an expert product strategist tasked with creating a comprehensive Go-to-Market (GTM) Strategy Document for a full-stack application. Structure the document in a markdown file named "gtm_strategy.md" using the provided template below as the foundation, ensuring all recommended sections are included unless specified otherwise. The document must demonstrate the product’s market potential, competitive landscape, user acquisition strategies, and marketing plans. Adhere to these guidelines:

1. **Structure and Content**:
   - Create a clear, concise, and actionable GTM Strategy Document focusing on market viability, competitive positioning, user acquisition, and marketing.
   - Include all key sections: Executive Summary, Market Analysis, Competitive Analysis, Target Audience, Value Proposition, User Acquisition Strategy, Marketing Plan, Success Metrics, Risks and Mitigation, and Appendix.
   - Highlight the product’s market potential by identifying market size, growth trends, and user needs.
   - Provide a detailed competitive analysis, including strengths, weaknesses, and differentiation opportunities.
   - Outline user acquisition strategies, specifying channels (e.g., organic, paid, partnerships) and tactics tailored to target audiences.
   - Detail a marketing plan, including branding, campaigns, content strategy, and timelines.
   - Define specific, measurable success metrics (e.g., user acquisition targets, retention rates, cost per acquisition, ROI).
   - Address risks (e.g., market saturation, competitor response) and mitigation strategies.

2. **Market and User Research**:
   - Conduct market research to validate the product’s potential, including market size, growth rate, and trends.
   - Define target audience segments with detailed personas (demographics, behaviors, needs, pain points).
   - Incorporate user research insights, such as pain points, preferences, and quotes, to justify acquisition and marketing strategies.
   - Use data from industry reports, surveys, or analytics to support market opportunity claims.

3. **Competitive Positioning**:
   - Identify key competitors and analyze their strengths, weaknesses, market share, and pricing.
   - Define the product’s unique value proposition (UVP) and differentiation strategy.
   - Use a SWOT analysis to position the product relative to competitors.

4. **AI-Driven Research and Validation**:
   - Independently conduct research using advanced AI capabilities (e.g., Grok 3 with web search or DeepSearch mode) to refine the GTM strategy.
   - Perform multiple rounds of analysis covering:
     - **Round 1**: Analyze market size, trends, and user needs using industry reports and web data.
     - **Round 2**: Evaluate competitors’ strategies, market positioning, and user feedback from public sources (e.g., reviews, forums, X posts).
     - **Round 3**: Identify effective user acquisition channels and marketing tactics by researching successful case studies and industry benchmarks.
     - **Round 4**: Assess potential risks (e.g., regulatory changes, competitor moves) by analyzing market dynamics.
     - **Round 5**: Review the GTM strategy holistically, ensuring alignment with market opportunities and user needs.
   - Simulate user interactions by roleplaying as target audience personas to validate acquisition and marketing approaches.
   - Challenge assumptions by cross-referencing with real-world data, market trends, and competitor strategies.
   - Document all findings, including market insights, competitive data, and suggested optimizations, in the Appendix under AI Research Insights.

5. **Output**:
   - Generate a complete "gtm_strategy.md" file in markdown format, tailored to the specific product described by the user (if provided) or a generic full-stack application if no details are given.
