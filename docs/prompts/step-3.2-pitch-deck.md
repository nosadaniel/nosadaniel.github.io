You are an expert startup strategist tasked with creating a compelling Pitch Deck for a full-stack application, designed for a solo entrepreneur or early-stage startup to present to investors, partners, or stakeholders. Structure the document in a markdown file named "pitch_deck.md" using the provided template below as the foundation, ensuring all recommended sections (slides) are included unless specified otherwise. The Pitch Deck must concisely convey the product vision, market opportunity, competitive edge, business model, and growth plan, aligning with the product’s PRD, GTM Strategy, Lean Startup Canvas, Technical Architecture Document, and UX Design Document. Adhere to these guidelines:

1. **Structure and Content**:
   - Create a concise, persuasive Pitch Deck with 10-12 slides, each focusing on a key aspect of the startup’s value proposition and potential.
   - Include all key slides: Cover, Problem, Solution, Market Opportunity, Product, Competitive Landscape, Business Model, Go-to-Market Strategy, Traction, Team, Financials, Ask, and Appendix.
   - **Cover**: Introduce the product and company with a tagline and branding aligned with the GTM Strategy.
   - **Problem**: Highlight customer pain points from the Lean Startup Canvas and PRD, emphasizing urgency and scale.
   - **Solution**: Present the product as the answer, referencing the PRD’s key features and UX Design Document’s user benefits.
   - **Market Opportunity**: Quantify market size and growth potential, drawing from the GTM Strategy’s market analysis.
   - **Product**: Showcase the product’s value proposition and UI, leveraging the UX Design Document and PRD.
   - **Competitive Landscape**: Position the product against competitors, using the GTM Strategy’s competitive analysis and Lean Startup Canvas’s unfair advantage.
   - **Business Model**: Detail revenue streams and pricing, aligned with the Lean Startup Canvas.
   - **Go-to-Market Strategy**: Summarize user acquisition and marketing plans from the GTM Strategy.
   - **Traction**: Highlight early wins (e.g., prototypes, user tests, pre-signups), even if minimal, to show progress.
   - **Team**: Introduce the founder(s) and key contributors, emphasizing relevant expertise and passion.
   - **Financials**: Provide high-level projections (revenue, costs, funding needs) based on the Lean Startup Canvas’s cost structure and revenue streams.
   - **Ask**: Specify funding or partnership needs, including use of funds and milestones.
   - Keep each slide concise (3-5 bullet points or a single compelling visual description), avoiding jargon and focusing on impact.

2. **Persuasive and Visual Design**:
   - Craft a narrative that connects the problem to the solution, market opportunity, and business potential, appealing to investor priorities (e.g., ROI, scalability).
   - Describe visuals (e.g., screenshots, charts, infographics) for each slide, noting that actual graphics will be created separately.
   - Ensure branding consistency with the GTM Strategy and UX Design Document’s visual design (e.g., color scheme, typography).
   - Prioritize clarity and emotional appeal to engage non-technical audiences.

3. **Alignment with Existing Documents**:
   - Draw user needs and product features from the PRD and UX Design Document to ensure the solution is grounded in user research.
   - Use the GTM Strategy’s market analysis, competitive positioning, and acquisition plans to support market and growth claims.
   - Reference the Lean Startup Canvas’s UVP, revenue streams, and unfair advantage to articulate business viability.
   - Align technical feasibility claims with the Technical Architecture Document’s stack and scalability plans.
   - Tailor the deck to a startup’s early stage, emphasizing vision and potential over extensive traction.

4. **AI-Driven Research and Validation**:
   - Independently conduct research using advanced AI capabilities (e.g., Grok 3 with web search or DeepSearch mode) to refine the Pitch Deck.
   - Perform multiple rounds of analysis covering:
     - **Round 1**: Validate market size and trends using industry reports, X posts, and public data.
     - **Round 2**: Analyze competitor strengths, weaknesses, and funding to refine positioning, leveraging GTM Strategy insights.
     - **Round 3**: Research successful startup pitch decks and investor preferences to optimize slide content and structure.
     - **Round 4**: Assess financial projections by benchmarking against similar startups’ revenue models and costs.
     - **Round 5**: Review the deck holistically, ensuring a cohesive narrative and data-driven claims.
   - Simulate investor perspectives by roleplaying as different stakeholder types (e.g., VC, angel investor) to test clarity and persuasiveness.
   - Challenge assumptions (e.g., market size, competitor threats) by cross-referencing with real-world data and industry trends.
   - Document all findings, including market insights, competitor data, and suggested optimizations, in the Appendix under AI Research Insights.

5. **Output**:
   - Generate a complete "pitch_deck.md" file in markdown format, tailored to the specific product described by the user (if provided) or a generic full-stack application if no details are given.
