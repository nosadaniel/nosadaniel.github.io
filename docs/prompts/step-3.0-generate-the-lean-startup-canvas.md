You are an expert startup strategist tasked with creating a Lean Startup Canvas for a full-stack application, designed for a solo entrepreneur or early-stage startup. Structure the document in a markdown file named "lean_canvas.md" using the provided template below, based on the Lean Canvas model by Ash Maurya. The canvas must consolidate the business strategy, covering problem, solution, unique value proposition, customer segments, channels, revenue streams, cost structure, key metrics, and unfair advantage. Adhere to these guidelines:

1. **Structure and Content**:
   - Create a concise, actionable Lean Startup Canvas that captures the business strategy on a single page.
   - Include all nine Lean Canvas components: Problem, Customer Segments, Unique Value Proposition, Solution, Channels, Revenue Streams, Cost Structure, Key Metrics, and Unfair Advantage.
   - Focus on validating assumptions and prioritizing the most critical elements for a startup to build and launch the product.
   - Align the canvas with the product’s PRD (features and requirements) and GTM Strategy (market and acquisition plans) if provided.
   - Identify the top 1-3 problems faced by target customers, linking them to user needs from the GTM Strategy.
   - Define clear customer segments, referencing personas from the GTM Strategy.
   - Articulate a compelling unique value proposition (UVP) that differentiates the product from competitors.
   - Outline a high-level solution tied to the PRD’s key features.
   - Specify channels for reaching customers, consistent with the GTM Strategy’s acquisition and marketing plans.
   - Detail revenue streams (e.g., subscriptions, freemium, one-time sales) and cost structure (e.g., development, marketing, hosting).
   - Define key metrics to track success (e.g., user growth, revenue, retention), aligning with GTM Strategy metrics.
   - Highlight an unfair advantage (e.g., proprietary technology, exclusive partnerships) that gives the startup a competitive edge.

2. **Customer and Market Validation**:
   - Validate customer problems and segments using insights from user research, surveys, or market data.
   - Ensure the UVP and solution address real customer pain points, supported by quotes or analytics if available.
   - Cross-reference market size and trends from the GTM Strategy to confirm viability.

3. **Financial and Operational Focus**:
   - Propose realistic revenue streams and cost estimates, considering a solo/startup’s limited resources.
   - Identify operational needs (e.g., outsourcing, tools) to support product development and launch.
   - Highlight funding requirements, if any, to achieve initial milestones.

4. **AI-Driven Research and Validation**:
   - Independently conduct research using advanced AI capabilities (e.g., Grok 3 with web search or DeepSearch mode) to refine the Lean Canvas.
   - Perform multiple rounds of analysis covering:
     - **Round 1**: Validate customer problems and segments by researching user feedback, forums, and X posts.
     - **Round 2**: Confirm market viability and revenue potential using industry reports and competitor pricing models.
     - **Round 3**: Evaluate channels and unfair advantage by analyzing successful startups in the same space.
     - **Round 4**: Identify risks (e.g., high customer acquisition costs, technical challenges) and propose mitigations.
     - **Round 5**: Review the canvas holistically, ensuring all components are cohesive and data-driven.
   - Simulate customer interactions by roleplaying as target segments to test the UVP and solution fit.
   - Challenge assumptions by cross-referencing with market trends, competitor strategies, and real-world data.
   - Document all findings, including customer insights, market data, and suggested optimizations, in the Appendix under AI Research Insights.

5. **Output**:
   - Generate a complete "lean_canvas.md" file in markdown format, tailored to the specific product described by the user (if provided) or a generic full-stack application if no details are given.
   - Use the following template as the starting point, filling in realistic placeholders if specific details are missing.
   - Ensure the canvas is concise, actionable, and adaptable for iterative refinement.
